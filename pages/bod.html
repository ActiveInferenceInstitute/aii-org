Board of Directors

Governance, strategy, and stewardship of the Institute's mission across education, research, and community.

Current membership

The Board of Directors is the Institute's primary governing body. It sets strategy, ensures fiduciary and legal compliance, approves major policies including the Bylaws, and holds ultimate accountability for the Institute's mission, alongside the Officers and the Scientific Advisory Board.

Current membership is maintained in the Institute's registry and published on the Institute's structure pages. That published roster is the canonical membership record; this page links it rather than repeating a list that can fall out of step.
