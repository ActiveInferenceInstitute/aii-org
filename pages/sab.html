Scientific Advisory Board

Guiding the Institute's research direction and academic rigour across neuroscience, AI, philosophy, and complex systems.

Current membership

The Scientific Advisory Board is a group of external experts who advise the Institute on scientific and technical matters. Its members help guide research priorities, uphold rigour across the Institute's programmes and publications, and connect the Institute to the broader scientific community, complementing the Board of Directors and the Officers.

The SAB is re-formed each year. Current membership and the recorded 2022–2025 cohorts are maintained in the Institute's registry and published on the Institute's structure pages. That published roster is the canonical membership record; this page links it rather than repeating a list that can fall out of step.
